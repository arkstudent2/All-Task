Communication

Communication is the process of sending and receiving messages to share information, ideas, and feelings between people

Types of Communication

Verbal Communication: Baat cheet ya speaking ke zariye communication
Written Communication: Emails, letters, messages aur reports ke zariye
Non-Verbal Communication: Facial expressions, body language aur gestures
Visual Communication: Images, charts, symbols aur presentations ke zariye.

Communication Process

Communication ke basic steps: Sender – Jo message bhejta hai
Message – Jo information share ki jati hai
Encoding – Message ko words, signs ya symbols mein convert karna
Channel – Jis medium se message bheja jata hai
Receiver – Jo message receive karta hai
Decoding – Message ka meaning samajhna
Feedback – Receiver ka response. 
Ideas aur information share karne mein help karti hai.
Teamwork ko improve karti hai.
Misunderstandings kam karti hai.
Relationships ko strong banati hai.
Education aur business mein important role play karti hai.
Problems ko solve karne mein madad deti hai.

Communication is the meaningful process of exchanging ideas, thoughts, feelings, knowledge, and information between people through words, actions, symbols, gestures, or other forms of expression. It is not merely the act of speaking or writing; rather, it is the art of creating understanding between individuals. Effective communication builds relationships, removes misunderstandings, inspires cooperation, and connects people emotionally and intellectually.
