• Field Marshal ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

A Field Marshal is the highest military rank in an army in countries that use this rank. It is generally considered a five-star rank, higher than the rank of General. A Field Marshal is a highly senior military officer who may be responsible for major military strategy, national defence, and the overall leadership of armed forces. The rank is usually awarded to individuals who have demonstrated exceptional leadership, bravery, and distinguished military service.
