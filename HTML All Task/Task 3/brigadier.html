• Brigadier ⭐

A Brigadier is a senior military officer who commands a brigade, which is a large military formation consisting of several battalions. In many armies, the rank of Brigadier is above Colonel and below Major General. A Brigadier is responsible for planning operations, leading troops, maintaining discipline, and ensuring that soldiers and equipment are properly organized and prepared for missions. The rank is commonly represented by one star in some military systems, although insignia vary between countries.
