• General ⭐⭐⭐⭐

A General is a very high-ranking military officer who holds a senior position in an army. It is usually a four-star rank and is below the rank of Field Marshal in countries that have that rank. A General may command large military formations, make important strategic decisions, and help plan national defence operations. Generals are responsible for leading soldiers, managing military resources, and ensuring discipline and readiness within their commands. The rank is awarded to experienced officers who demonstrate strong leadership, professional knowledge, courage, and dedication.
