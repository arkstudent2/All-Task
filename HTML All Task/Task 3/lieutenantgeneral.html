• Lieutenant General ⭐⭐⭐

A Lieutenant General is a senior military officer who holds a three-star rank in the army. It is below the rank of General and above Major General. A Lieutenant General usually commands a large military formation, such as a corps, and is responsible for leading thousands of soldiers. They help develop military strategies, supervise operations, maintain discipline, and manage important resources. Lieutenant Generals are experienced officers who have demonstrated strong leadership, professional skills, courage, and dedication to their country.
