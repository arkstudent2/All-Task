• Major General ⭐⭐

A Major General is a senior military officer who holds a two-star rank in the army. It is below the rank of Lieutenant General and above Brigadier. A Major General may command a large military formation, such as a division, and is responsible for leading thousands of soldiers. They help plan military operations, manage personnel and resources, maintain discipline, and ensure that their units remain trained and prepared. Major Generals are experienced officers who have demonstrated strong leadership, professional knowledge, courage, and dedication to their country.
