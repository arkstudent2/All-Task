LAPTOP

A laptop is a portable personal computer that is designed to be used easily in different places. Unlike a desktop computer, a laptop has a built-in screen, keyboard, touchpad, speakers, battery, processor, memory, and storage in a compact and lightweight body. It can perform many of the same tasks as a desktop computer, but it is easier to carry and use. Laptops are commonly used for education, office work, business, programming, internet browsing, online meetings, entertainment, and communication. They can run different operating systems and software applications, allowing users to create documents, prepare presentations, edit photos and videos, browse websites, and store important files. One of the main advantages of a laptop is its portability. It can operate using a rechargeable battery, so users can work without being connected to a power source for a certain period of time. Modern laptops also provide wireless connectivity such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, making it easy to connect to the internet and other devices.
