COMPUTER

A computer is an electronic machine that is designed to accept data as input, process that data according to a set of instructions, store it for future use, and produce meaningful information as output. Computers can perform millions of operations in a very short time and are capable of handling large amounts of data with high speed and accuracy. A computer consists of both hardware and software. Hardware includes physical components such as the keyboard, mouse, monitor, CPU, printer, and storage devices, while software consists of programs and instructions that tell the computer what to do. Modern computers are used in almost every field, including education, business, banking, healthcare, communication, science, engineering, entertainment, and government offices. Computers have made our daily lives easier by helping us perform complex tasks quickly and efficiently. They can be used to create documents and presentations, calculate information, store important files, communicate with people, access the internet, watch videos, play games, and perform many other activities.
