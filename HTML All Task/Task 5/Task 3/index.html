Verbal Intelligence Test

A Verbal Intelligence Test is a comprehensive psychometric assessment specifically engineered to evaluate a person's overall cognitive capability to analyze information, reason logically, and solve complex problems through language, words, numbers, and symbolic concepts. Rather than testing memorized factual knowledge or specialized academic subjects, this evaluation measures raw mental processing power, verbal comprehension, linguistic agility, and abstract reasoning skills. It functions on the principle that an individual's capacity to understand, process, and manipulate verbal and symbolic information directly reflects their problem-solving efficiency, communication potential, and ability to adapt rapidly under pressure. Read more

Non-Verbal Intelligence Test

A Non-Verbal Intelligence Test is a psychometric evaluation designed to measure an individual's abstract reasoning, spatial awareness, and cognitive problem-solving abilities without relying on written or spoken language. Instead of using words, vocabulary, or text-based scenarios, this test relies entirely on visual elements such as geometric shapes, patterns, symbols, matrices, and visual sequences. It operates on the core principle of assessing pure fluid intelligence—the capacity to think logically, analyze unfamiliar visual information, identify underlying rules, and solve complex problems independently of formal education, language background, or cultural learning.  Read more

General Knowledge & Current Affairs

General Knowledge & Current Affairs refers to a vital evaluation domain in competitive military screening examinations, such as the Pakistan Military Academy PMA Long Course initial academic test and interview phase, designed to measure a candidate's broader understanding of the world, historical context, and contemporary socio-political environment. General Knowledge encompasses a vast repository of foundational factual information spanning world geography, international organizations, national defense milestones, historical events, scientific discoveries, Islamic history, and key national affairs. Current Affairs complements this by assessing an applicant's active awareness of recent global and domestic developments, including ongoing political dynamics, foreign policy updates, military agreements, economic trends, and significant international occurrences. Together, this subject area evaluates an individual's intellectual curiosity, mental maturity, situational awareness, and capacity to stay informed about national and international shifts, serving as a critical indicator of an candidate's readiness for strategic thinking and military leadership. Read more

Academic Test (Physics, Mathematics & English)

The Academic Test covering Physics, Mathematics, and English is a essential component of the initial computerized screening examination for candidates applying to the Pakistan Military Academy PMA Long Course. Designed to assess a candidate's academic competence based on the Higher Secondary School Certificate curriculum, this assessment evaluates fundamental scientific concepts, quantitative problem-solving skills, and linguistic proficiency required for higher military education. The Physics section tests core principles such as mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and wave motion, ensuring candidates possess strong analytical comprehension of physical phenomena. The Mathematics portion focuses on algebraic equations, trigonometry, calculus, geometry, and numerical logic to measure problem-solving speed and mathematical reasoning. Concurrently, the English evaluation measures structural language skills through grammar rules, vocabulary, sentence structure, prepositions, and active-passive conversions to ensure clear comprehension and communication abilities. Collectively, this multi-disciplinary test verifies that candidates maintain a solid educational foundation necessary to undergo the rigorous technical, academic, and tactical training at the military academy.    Read more

Selection Procedure & ISSB Overview

The Selection Procedure & ISSB Overview refers to the structured, multi-phase recruitment process used to evaluate, screen, and select potential officer cadets for the Pakistan Military Academy PMA Long Course. The journey begins at the Army Selection and Recruitment Centres with preliminary computerized intelligence and academic tests, followed by initial medical screening, physical tests—such as running, push-ups, sit-ups, chin-ups, and ditch crossing—and an initial interview. Shortlisted candidates proceed to the Inter Services Selection Board for an intensive four-day residential evaluation, where outdoor group tasks, obstacle courses, psychological assessments, word association tests, situational reactions, and personal interviews measure leadership potential, stress management, decision-making ability, and overall officer-like qualities. Candidates recommended by the ISSB then undergo a final detailed medical examination at Combined Military Hospitals before General Headquarters issues the final merit list for academy admission. Read more
